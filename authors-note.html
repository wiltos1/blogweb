Why This Site Exists
🏠Home

Thank you Mom for maintaining and updating this blog for the past 20 years. We all love reading your new posts and looking back at the many trips, milestones, and everyday moments that make up our family story. It’s such a gift that you’ve taken the time to preserve these memories for all of us.

This site exists to make it easier and more fun to explore everything you’ve written about. It brings together posts, photos, and moments from across the years in one place, so we can search, browse, and rediscover our adventures whenever we want. Some fun features include a world map of locations we've travelled to, a slideshow builder (my personal favourite), AI based post searching and much more.

I had a lot of fun making this, and I hope you'll enjoy using it to relive all our old memories and fun times we've had.

Happy 50th Birthday. With love,
Sam
